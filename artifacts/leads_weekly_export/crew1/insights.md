# Insights - who converts and when

_Narrative written by: offline template. Numbers computed by the EDA tool._

## Business summary

- We received **5,000 leads** (419/month); **12.8%** of closed leads enrolled. **143 leads are still open** and are today's call candidates.
- **Speed to lead matters most:** leads called within < 1h convert at 16.3% vs 4.7% when the first call comes after 3 days.
- **Channel quality differs:** referral leads convert at 24.3%, facebook_form at 8.2% - volume is not value.
- **Webinar attendees** convert at 23.2% vs 8.4% for non-attendees.
- Highest-converting course interest: **data_analyst** (13.9%).
- **Recommendation:** stop calling in arrival order. Call the newest high-probability leads first, within the first hours, and open with what they wrote in the form.
